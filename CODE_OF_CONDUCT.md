# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

KAIROSEED aims to be a technical project where people can discuss governance, security, verification, and agentic systems constructively and respectfully.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific.
- Critique implementations and evidence, not people.
- Distinguish facts, observations, hypotheses, and opinions.
- Preserve failed artifacts when they are relevant to verification.
- Do not claim a security property without evidence.
- Do not intentionally disclose secrets or sensitive information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, malicious disclosure of private information, and deliberate attempts to misrepresent test results are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors and the integrity of the project. Security issues should follow `SECURITY.md`.
