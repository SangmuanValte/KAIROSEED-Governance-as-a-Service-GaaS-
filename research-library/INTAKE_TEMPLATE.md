## Mandatory review gate

A `VERIFIED_SCOPE` promotion requires concrete supporting evidence, exact tested scope, an independent observer, identified revision, and an accountable human decision. Keep contradictory or failed evidence linked. `UNVERIFIED` is not synonymous with FAIL, and `SPECIFIED` is not PASS.
